# Known / expected behaviours — do NOT report these

Things the bonus/product owners have confirmed are by design. Observing them is not a finding: don't add them to "Needs attention" and don't count them as warnings. Add new entries only when a bonus or product owner confirms a behaviour is expected; note the date.

| Behaviour | Why it's expected | Confirmed on |
|---|---|---|
| In Promoții și Bonusuri → Istoric, a used bonus's card shows the bonus **expiry date** (e.g. 21.11.2026) as its tag, not the date it was used | Per implementation; raised to product as an improvement, not a test finding. Applies to every bonus type | 2026-11-18; re-confirmed 2026-11-19 |
| **Countdown Free Spins:** after the countdown ends the bonus leaves "Bonusuri" but is missing from Istoric and `bondet_state` stays `inprogress`/`active` for ~10 minutes; it then flips to `complete` and shows in Istoric with the win | Known issue/delay by design; don't report if it settles within ~15 min. Wait at least 10-15 min before marking S3/S4 FAIL, and report only if it is still missing after that | 2026-11-18 |
| Bonus popups show the countdown in **seconds** ("3600 secunde") while the card name, T&C and other communications show minutes | Popups use seconds by design | 2026-11-18 |
| **Pragmatic games** open on an intro screen with a round play arrow (gorilla logo) that looks frozen until it is pressed; the CTA landing on this screen is normal | Normal Pragmatic behaviour, press play from the intro screen | 2026-11-18 |
| Countdown Free Spins award popup shows the duration with decimals, e.g. "You got **60.00** seconds of Free Spins" | Controlled on the provider side; we can't change it. Don't report it at all (not even as Cosmetic) | 2026-11-24 |
| **EGT/Amusnet Countdown Free Spins:** the game's own Time left timer starts when the player presses PLAY NOW on the game's Countdown Free Spins popup, before the first spin. Betler (and the bonus card) start the countdown at the first spin, so the game can end the free spins earlier than Betler expects (typically 15–20 s early) | Known implementation gap, being raised with product. Don't fail the bonus or count it as a check failure. Mark the clock-start checks ✅ and add one line in "Not tested / caveats": known gap, with the measured difference | 2026-11-24 |
| **Free Chips payout by provider:** Evolution free chips pay the **gross** return of a winning bet (stake + win, e.g. €10 on red that wins credits €20). Pragmatic free chips pay **net** (win only, e.g. €0.50 chip that wins credits €0.50) | Provider behaviour by design. Check Evolution = gross and Pragmatic = net; flag the opposite (Evolution net or Pragmatic gross) as a finding | 2026-11-23 |

**General rule:** don't raise as a finding anything the team can't change because the provider controls it (popup wording or number formats generated by the provider). If unsure whether something is provider-side, mention it once in the caveats as "likely provider-side", not as a warning.

If a behaviour looks like one of these but differs (e.g. the date shown is neither the expiry nor the usage date, or is wrong versus the configured expiry), that **is** a finding — compare against the expected rule, not just the pattern.
